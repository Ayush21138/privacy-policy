Your Privacy Matters to Us

PartsBaba.ai is built around helping customers source the parts they need. To make that possible, we sometimes need information from you. We aim to collect information that is relevant to our services, use it responsibly and be transparent about how it is handled.

🔎

What We Collect

Information that helps us provide RFQ, sourcing, account and support services.

⚙️

Why We Use It

To process requirements, communicate with you, arrange services and improve PartsBaba.ai.

🛡️

How We Protect It

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle.

01

Welcome to PartsBaba.ai

At PartsBaba.ai, we believe sourcing should be simple, transparent and reliable. We also believe that your personal information should be handled responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use PartsBaba.ai, how we use it, how we may share it and the choices available to you.

By accessing or using PartsBaba.ai, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use PartsBaba.ai, we may collect information that helps us provide our sourcing, RFQ and support services.

Information You Provide

When you submit an RFQ, create an account, contact us or otherwise communicate with our team, you may provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or organisation name
  • Billing or shipping information
  • Product and part requirements
  • Quantity requirements
  • Delivery preferences
  • Information included in your RFQ
  • Messages or other information you choose to share with us
  • Account and login information, where applicable
We focus on relevant information.

We aim to request information that is reasonably relevant to providing our services and communicating with you.

03

Information Collected Through Website Usage

When you visit or interact with PartsBaba.ai, certain technical information may be collected automatically.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Network connection information
  • Pages visited
  • Website interaction information
  • Approximate session or usage information
  • Date and time of access
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the website

This information helps us operate the website, understand how it is being used, improve performance and support website security.

04

Information From Your RFQ

When you submit a Request for Quote, the information you provide may be used by PartsBaba to understand and process your requirement.

We may use RFQ information to:

  • Identify suitable products
  • Contact relevant suppliers
  • Check product availability
  • Request pricing
  • Compare sourcing options
  • Prepare a quotation
  • Communicate expected lead times
  • Arrange procurement and delivery
  • Contact you regarding your requirement

Some RFQs may contain technical or product-specific information. We use this information to help source the requested products and services.

05

Information We May Receive From Third Parties

We may receive certain information from third parties that support PartsBaba.ai or help us provide our services.

These may include:

  • Technology and website service providers
  • Communication service providers
  • Analytics and measurement providers
  • Payment or transaction service providers, where applicable
  • Logistics or fulfilment partners
  • Other service providers working on our behalf

We may also receive information through online technologies such as cookies, pixels, web beacons, software libraries or similar technologies where these are used on our services.

Information received from third parties is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.

06

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Process and respond to RFQs
  • Prepare quotations
  • Communicate with customers
  • Source products through our supplier network
  • Process and manage orders
  • Arrange shipping and delivery
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain customer accounts
  • Improve PartsBaba.ai and its services
  • Understand website usage and user interaction
  • Maintain website security
  • Detect, investigate and address possible fraudulent, illegal or malicious activity
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements
  • Communicate important service-related updates

We aim to use information for purposes that are relevant to your interaction with PartsBaba.

07

Cookies & Similar Technologies

PartsBaba.ai may use cookies or similar technologies to help the website function properly and improve your experience.

Cookies and similar technologies may help us:

  • Keep the website functional
  • Remember certain preferences
  • Understand website usage
  • Improve website performance
  • Measure engagement
  • Support website security
  • Understand interactions with communications or advertisements, where applicable

You may be able to manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Some website functionality may be affected if certain cookies are disabled or restricted.

08

How We Share Information

PartsBaba may share relevant information with trusted third parties when this is necessary to provide requested services, operate the platform, fulfil an order or meet applicable requirements.

Suppliers & Sourcing Partners

When required to source a product, relevant RFQ information may be shared with suppliers or sourcing partners so they can help us check availability, pricing and product details.

Logistics & Delivery Partners

Where required to fulfil an order, relevant delivery information may be shared with logistics, shipping or fulfilment partners.

Technology & Service Providers

We may work with providers that support website hosting, IT management, communications, analytics, customer support, cloud storage and other business functions.

Business & Marketing Partners

Where applicable and permitted, we may work with business or marketing partners to provide services, communicate relevant offers or improve the experience of our users.

Legal & Regulatory Requirements

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or a valid governmental request, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights and security of PartsBaba, our users or others.

Our approach to sharing information

We aim to share information only where it is relevant to providing our services, operating the platform, fulfilling your request or meeting applicable requirements.

09

Categories of Information We May Handle

Depending on how you interact with PartsBaba.ai, the information we handle may fall into categories such as:

  • Identifiers: such as name, email address, phone number and account information.
  • Commercial information: such as RFQ details, product requirements, quotations, order information and customer support information.
  • Internet or network activity: such as IP address, browser information, device information and website interaction data.
  • Approximate location information: where location information may be derived from technical data such as an IP address.
  • Communication information: such as messages, enquiries and support communications you choose to send to us.

We do not seek to collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary, permitted and appropriately handled under applicable law.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Our security practices may include appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect information used in connection with PartsBaba.ai.

Security is an ongoing process.

We continuously aim to maintain appropriate safeguards as our platform, technology and services evolve.

No website, online service or method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend avoiding insecure channels when communicating sensitive or confidential information.

11

How Long We Keep Information

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Depending on the circumstances, information may be retained to:

  • Manage customer relationships
  • Maintain RFQ and order records
  • Provide support
  • Complete accounting and business records
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce applicable agreements and policies
  • Protect the rights and security of PartsBaba and its users

When information is no longer required, we may delete it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable requirements.

12

Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

These may include:

  • Access / Know: Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Portability: Request a copy of personal information in circumstances where applicable law provides this right.
  • Restriction: Ask us to restrict or stop certain processing where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may be able to withdraw that consent.
  • Communication Preferences: Opt out of promotional communications using the unsubscribe option provided or by contacting us.

These rights may not apply in every situation and may be subject to applicable legal exceptions.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain privacy requests.

Where permitted by applicable law, you may also be able to appoint an authorised person to make a request on your behalf.

Children's Privacy
